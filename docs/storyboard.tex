{\Large\bfseries\color{deep} Storyboard: public EV charging in France, where it lags and how fast the gap is closing}\\[3pt]
{\color{muted} Oscar Masdupuy, EFREI Paris, BIA. Data Visualization 2026. Figures from the data of \nBuildDate.} {\small\itshape Formatting with the help of AI.}

\vspace{8pt}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}L{3.2cm}Y@{}}
\textbf{Audience} & Département and regional planners who write or revise a local charging master plan (SDIRVE, created by the 2019 LOM law). \\
\textbf{Questions} & Where is public charging thin once population and EV ownership are taken into account? Is the gap between départements closing? \\
\textbf{Hypothesis tested} & Deployment sped up after 2021 but stayed in large metropolitan areas and on motorways, leaving rural départements behind on fast charging. \\
\textbf{Takeaway} & The first half holds, the second does not. Rural mainland départements are level with dense ones, and the gap is closing. The gaps that remain are overseas and, per car, in dense départements around Paris. \\
\end{tabularx}
